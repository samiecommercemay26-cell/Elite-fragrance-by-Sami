About Elite Fragrance

A small shop with one big idea: everyone deserves to smell luxurious.

Hi, I'm Sami

I started Elite Fragrance because I love how a single scent can change how you walk into a room. Luxury perfume was too expensive for most people I knew, so I set out to find bottles that smell rich without the heavy price tag.

Today we sell luxury, royal and refreshing perfumes and impressions of popular designer scents. I try every fragrance myself before it goes on the shelf.

What we stand for

Quality first

Strong oils, clean blends and bottles that last for months, not weeks.

Honest value

Fair prices, clear descriptions and no pressure to buy.

Personal service

You talk to Sami directly. Tell him what you like and he will help you choose.

Our three collections

Luxury scents are warm, rich and made for special moments.

Royal scents are bold oud and spice blends with serious staying power.

Refreshing scents are light and crisp for daily wear and summer days.
